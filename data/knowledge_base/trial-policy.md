# Free Trial Policy

## 1. What the trial includes

New organizations can try **CloudBox Pro free for 14 days**. No payment method is required to start. One trial is available per organization (identified by the verified email domain).

During the trial you get Pro features with two limits: the monthly API quota is capped at **100,000 requests** and the API rate limit is **300 requests per minute**.

## 2. When the trial ends

* **No payment method on file:** the organization moves to the Free plan. Data above Free plan limits becomes read-only for 30 days and is then deleted unless you upgrade.
* **Payment method on file:** the trial **converts automatically to Pro with monthly billing ($49 / month)** at the end of the trial, and the first charge is made on the conversion date.

To avoid conversion, remove the payment method or cancel the trial in **Settings > Billing > Plan** before the trial ends. Cancelling a trial is immediate and free.

## 3. Refunds after conversion

If the trial converted and you did not intend to continue, you can request a full refund within **48 hours of the first charge**, provided there has been no usage since the conversion. See [Refund Policy §8](refund-policy.md#8-trial-conversions).

## 4. Extensions

Trials are not extended for self-serve customers. Organizations evaluating Business or Enterprise can request a one-time 7-day extension through the sales team.
