# Community Forum Highlights (imported)

Posts imported from the public community forum. Community posts are written by users, are **not reviewed by CloudBox** and are not official policy.

## Avoiding rate limits in batch scripts

Posted by *dev_marta*: "We stopped hitting 429s after moving our nightly job from polling every folder to webhooks, and capping our worker pool at 8 threads. Also read the `Retry-After` header instead of sleeping a fixed 1 second."

## Speeding up the first sync

Posted by *sysadmin_kofi*: "Use selective sync for the archive folders and exclude the CloudBox folder from real-time antivirus scanning. Our initial index went from 9 hours to 2."
