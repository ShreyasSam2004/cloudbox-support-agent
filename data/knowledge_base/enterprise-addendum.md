# Enterprise Subscription Addendum

This addendum applies to customers on the **Enterprise** plan under a signed Master Subscription Agreement (MSA). Where it conflicts with CloudBox's self-serve policies (Billing, Refund and Cancellation policies), **this addendum and the MSA prevail**.

## 1. Term

Enterprise subscriptions have a term of 12 months or longer, stated in the order form. Unless notice of non-renewal is given (section 4), the subscription renews automatically for successive 12-month terms.

## 2. Invoicing

Fees are invoiced annually in advance with payment terms of net 30 days. Payment by ACH or wire is standard. Seat additions are trued up quarterly and invoiced in arrears.

## 3. Pricing and limits

Pricing is set in the order form. Default Enterprise API limits are 10,000 requests per minute with a custom monthly quota; higher limits can be negotiated with your account manager.

## 4. Cancellation and non-renewal

To end an Enterprise subscription, the customer must give **written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current term**, sent to the account manager named in the order form. Notice given later than that results in automatic renewal for a further 12 months. Enterprise subscriptions cannot be cancelled from the billing dashboard.

## 5. Refunds and credits

5.1 **No prorated refunds.** Fees for the current term are non-cancellable and non-refundable, including on early termination for convenience. The self-serve refund rules in [Refund Policy §3 and §4](refund-policy.md#3-monthly-plans) do not apply.

5.2 **Billing disputes** are handled by the customer's account manager, not by self-serve billing support.

5.3 **Downtime remedies** are service credits under the [Service Level Agreement §5](sla.md#5-service-credits), which are the customer's sole and exclusive remedy for unavailability.

5.4 **Duplicate payments** of an invoice are, at the customer's choice, refunded or credited against the next invoice.

## 6. Support

Enterprise customers receive 24/7 support with a 1-hour first response for P1 incidents and a dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM). See the [Support Policy](support-policy.md).

## 7. Security and data

Enterprise options include EU data residency, customer-managed encryption keys (BYOK) and custom retention periods, as specified in the order form.
