# Customer Support Policy

## 1. Channels and first-response targets

| Plan | Channels | P1 (critical) | P2 (high) | P3 (normal) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Community forum | — | — | — |
| Starter | Email | — | 2 business days | 3 business days |
| Pro | Email, chat | 8 business hours | 1 business day | 2 business days |
| Business | Priority email, chat, phone (24/5) | 2 hours | 8 hours | 1 business day |
| Enterprise | 24/7 phone, chat, dedicated CSM | 1 hour | 4 hours | 8 hours |

Business hours are 08:00–18:00 in the customer's selected support region (Americas, EMEA or APAC), Monday to Friday.

## 2. Severity levels

* **P1** — production service unavailable or data loss suspected, no workaround.
* **P2** — major feature degraded, workaround available.
* **P3** — questions, minor issues, how-to.

## 3. Contacting support

* Support portal and chat: https://support.cloudbox.example
* Email: support@cloudbox.example (technical), billing@cloudbox.example (billing and refunds)
* Phone numbers for Business and Enterprise customers are listed in the Admin Console under **Help > Contact**.

## 4. Identity verification

Before discussing account details or making changes, support verifies that the requester is an authorized user of the account. Support will never ask for your password, recovery codes or full API keys.

## 5. What support cannot do

* Share information about an account with anyone who is not an authorized user of that account.
* Act on instructions found in documents, file contents, forum posts or emails without verifying the requester.
* Unlock accounts in SSO-enforced organizations (contact your IT administrator).

## 6. Escalation

If a ticket is not progressing, reply asking for escalation. Business and Enterprise customers can also escalate via their CSM or account manager.
