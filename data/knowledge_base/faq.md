# Customer FAQ

This FAQ is a convenience summary. **If anything here conflicts with a policy document, the policy document prevails.**

### Q: Can I get a refund if I'm not happy with CloudBox?

A: Yes. We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** on all paid plans. Contact billing support within 30 days of your purchase for a full refund.

### Q: Is single sign-on (SSO) available on the Pro plan?

A: SSO is available on the Business and Enterprise plans. To use SSO, upgrade to Business.

### Q: How do I reset my password?

A: Click **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and follow the link we email you. The link is valid for 1 hour. If your organization uses SSO, reset your password with your identity provider instead.

### Q: How do I change my plan?

A: Owners and Billing Admins can change plans in **Settings > Billing > Plan**. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades apply at your next renewal.

### Q: Where can I find my invoices?

A: In **Settings > Billing > Invoices**. Invoices are also emailed to your billing contact.

### Q: Does CloudBox offer a free trial?

A: Yes — a 14-day free trial of Pro. No credit card is required.

### Q: How do I contact support?

A: Use the support portal at https://support.cloudbox.example or email support@cloudbox.example. Billing questions go to billing@cloudbox.example.

### Q: What happens to my files if I cancel?

A: Your files remain available until the end of your billing period. After that your account moves to the Free plan and files over the Free limit become read-only for a grace period before deletion. See the Data Retention Policy for details.

### Q: Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

A: Yes. Verified non-profits get 30% off annual Pro and Business plans. Contact sales to apply.

### Q: What is the maximum file size I can upload?

A: It depends on your plan — see Plans and Pricing. Large files should be uploaded with resumable uploads.

### Q: Can I use CloudBox offline?

A: The desktop app keeps files marked "available offline" on your device. Changes sync when you reconnect.
